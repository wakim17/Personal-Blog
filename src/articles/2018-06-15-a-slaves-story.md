> Dark, Cold, Night
>
> It is full of fright.
>
> I miss my children and my wife.
>
> What has happened to my once young and happy life?
>
> I feel so depressed.
>
> All running towards the west.
>
> Will we see our tribe?
>
> My feelings and pain I cannot describe.
>
> I want to go back home
>
> and end my feelings of being alone.
>
> And get out of here quickly
>
> Before we all get stoned.
>
>  
>
> Sailing far from my home,
>
> travelling to the land of the unknown.
>
> Scared and lonely, I can onlywait,
>
> For the white man, I am filled with hate.                                                                        
>
> Sold to the white man
>
> Captured in metal shackles
>
> Work all day and night
>
> Hoping for a battle
>
> Doing everything a soul can
>
> Get beat up with whips and knuckles 
>
> Unable to speak up, I'm full of fright
>
> Hoping this will settle
>
> *We aren't animals*
>
> *We aren't animals*
>
> Cotton, sugar cane, tobacco too
>
> Working and picking crops is all I do!
>
> Working hard until I bleed,
>
> My master's words I must heed.
>
>

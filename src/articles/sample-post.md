I was lying down in the corner of a gloomy, dark office, with the echoes of a coin I was tossing and my tears dropping onto my crotch. Paranoid of the hanging bloody bodies around me, dripping blood on the white ceramic floor, a sudden whisper caught my focus from across this tight space; a feminine voice with a cute but frightened tone whispered, *“Why did you do it?”* I directly stared at the woman’s eyes with a smile and a teary eye. I gushed with excitement. I’ll start from the beginning.

I know everything, everything that the world doesn’t know. I know the meaning of life; I wasn’t born a human, nor have I been created through sexual intercourse. Unidentified parents manufactured me in a classified location. That’s all I could remember until twenty years ago when I fought with the Nazis. I was a coward. I harmed and killed no one. I surrendered myself to humankind, who killed the people I once called friends and family, the allies. They were kind. They didn’t torture me. Instead, they let me flee the war to the United States, surprisingly not checking my real name. I told them it was Alfred Rudulf.

After the war ended with Hitler committing suicide, I was frightened. So I went to Berlin searching for my mother, not knowing that I would end up being picked on and beaten by English men. That’s when my mind clicked and kept getting thoughts of revenge; at that moment I knew how my creator had felt. I wanted chaos for the disrespecting weaklings. You call human beings. I want them to suffer and keep the girl that seemed different to me; keep her and make her understand that I am the person to take billions of souls for her good.

She replied, *“Was that a confession, or are you still irritated about your father?”* with her adorable voice. I giggled a little, then continued my story.

I planned to pretend I was a professional hacker, work unexpectedly with the government, brainwash the poor into brutally murdering the rich and order them to cage the women in one specific area, but that didn’t work. Right after that was said, a nuclear siren pierced into my ear, and then a colossal shockwave blew me against the wall. Luckily, I was protected by the metallic furniture, but with a bloody nose, I suddenly blacked out.

I woke up seconds later in a lab, where I saw scientists blasting bullets through their skulls and electric wires hanging from the ceiling shooting out static electricity. I looked past the hanging mannequins to examine the woman I was talking to. She became a sack of guts and skin in the opposite corner of the darkroom. I stared back at my floor, but this time smiling with delight, while my ears were ringing. Suddenly a dark figure came up to me with his hand ready to help me up. *“It's time for Fredrick Adolf Hitler to complete his father’s work.”*

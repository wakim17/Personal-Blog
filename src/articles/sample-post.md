The concept of a **digital garden** is rooted in the idea of cultivating knowledge over time. Unlike a traditional blog, which is chronologically sorted and performative, a garden is topically arranged and exploratory.

## Seeds of Thought

When we plant an idea, it begins as a seed—a rough draft, a passing thought, or a quote from an interesting book. Over time, as we revisit the note, we water it with new insights, link it to other concepts, and watch it grow into a fully formed essay.

> "A garden is never finished."

In the digital age, we suffer not from a lack of information, but from a lack of filtering and synthesis. We need spaces where we can construct meaning at our own pace. 

### Why the Garden Metaphor Works
1. **Growth over time:** Articles are not published and forgotten; they are updated and refined.
2. **Interconnectedness:** Like roots beneath the soil, ideas connect to one another through tags and links.
3. **Patience:** Wisdom requires time to digest information.

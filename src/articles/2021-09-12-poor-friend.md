> I’ve been thinking for a while 
>
> By while, I mean my whole life
>
> Wasted by fear, anger and depression
>
> Not knowing anything about my afterlife
>
> Got a gun next to me as an option 
>
> Got death next to me ready for adoption
>
> Got myself stuck in this situation
>
> Got no one to assist me with thecoordination
>
> Waiting for God to bring me an angel
>
> That angel would become my wife that lovesme 
>
> An angel that I would do anything to keep
>
> An angel who wouldn’t push me into a holeso deep
>
> Life feels like a nightmare with untoldfeelings 
>
> Sometimes you need to unleash them, to getthe proper healing
>
> I was talking to God while kneeing 
>
> I asked and prayed to him “I want to stopfalling”
>
> **\---------\*gunshot\*------------------**
>
> I heard a gunshot in my friend’s room
>
> I rushed in and looked down with a fume
>
> My friend laying in his own blood
>
> I laid next to him I'm tears while I staredat his guts
>
> I’m sorry for everything you’ve beenthrough 
>
> It must’ve been very hard on you 
>
> I’m sorry for all that’s been said and done
>
> I knew I should’ve never got a gun
>
> If only I could’ve been a better friend
>
> I’m sorry if it seemed like I didn’t care
>
> FUCK, you’re already gone that’s not fair.
>
> **\------------person2----------------------------**
>
> Hiding the hurt
>
> Hiding the pain 
>
> Hiding the tears 
>
> That fall like rain
>
> Saying I’m fine
>
> But I’m anything but 
>
> This ache in my soul
>
> Rips at my guts
>
> I lay in my bed after a long day of hate
>
> I lay there and cry while trying to debate
>
> There is not a lot I can say
>
> But things don’t go my way
>
> I cry until I have made up my mind.
>
> So I get up and walk forward not lookingbehind
>
> I grabbed the gun hidden under my bed
>
> Wishing I wouldn’t have left all myfeelings unsaid 
>
> I hold the gun unto my head
>
> …..here is my chance…….
>
> I wrap my fingers around the trigger
>
> ….just one more glance….
>
> I pull the trigger as hard as I can 
>
> Not worrying about my previous plan
>
> The bullet rushes through my temple into mybrain
>
> I fell to the floor with such enormous pain
>
> And now I’m done with this silly game
>
>  **\------gunshot-------**
>
> Before I faded into darkness, heard myfriend
>
> He thinks it’s his fault I shot myself
>
> Shit, why did I pull the trigger
>
> Please, man, help me, don’t call agravedigger
>
> I started begging to go back
>
> Gonna give my mama a heart attack
>
> Please god I wasn’t prepared 
>
> Shit, I’m cold, now I’m scared
>
> Mama save me I’m passing away 
>
> I wish I didn’t put that bullet in the back of my brain

 

# Please listen:

Do you want to die?

Throw yourself in the sea

And you will see yourself fighting tosurvive

You do not want to kill yourself

You want to kill something inside

Please don’t do it

Don’t ever permanently end your life

For a temporary problem

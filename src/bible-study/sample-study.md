> "Again Jesus spoke to them, saying, 'I am the light of the world. Whoever follows me will not walk in darkness, but will have the light of life.'" — **John 8:12 (ESV)**

## Context of the Feast of Tabernacles

When Jesus spoke these words in the Treasury of the Temple, the **Feast of Tabernacles (Sukkot)** was taking place. During this festival, four massive golden lampstands were lit in the Court of the Women, illuminating the entire city of Jerusalem. 

It was against this vivid backdrop of temporary golden lamps that Jesus declared Himself to be the true, everlasting **Light of the World**.

## Key Themes

1. **Illumination over Darkness**: Light exposes error and provides clear guidance on the path of truth.
2. **Followership**: Walking in the light requires active, continuous following of Christ.
3. **Life and Truth**: The light Christ gives is not merely intellectual knowledge; it imparts spiritual vitality.

## Personal Reflection & Application

* In what areas of my life am I still clinging to shadow instead of bringing them into Christ's light?
* How can we reflect Christ's illumination to those around us in our daily callings?
